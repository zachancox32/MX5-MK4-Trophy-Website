---
title: "The championship is now the MX-5 Mk4 Trophy"
date: 2026-10-14
category: News
excerpt: "A new name, a new identity, and a clearer statement of what the grid actually is."
---

The championship has been renamed the **MX-5 Mk4 Trophy**, replacing the
MX-5 Cup Great Britain name used previously.

The reason is simple. Every car on this grid is a Mk4 Mazda MX-5, built
to one sealed specification, and the name should say so. "Cup Great
Britain" told you where we race. "Mk4 Trophy" tells you what you race.

The new identity carries a yellow and red treatment across the badge and
wordmark, and it will appear on timing screens, grid boards and car
numbers from the start of the next season.

Nothing changes about how the championship runs. We continue under the
BRSCC, to the same technical regulations, on the same calendar of UK
circuits.
