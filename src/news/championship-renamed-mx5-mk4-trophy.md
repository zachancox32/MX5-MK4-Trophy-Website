---
title: "The championship is now the MX-5 Mk4 Trophy"
date: 2026-10-14
category: News
excerpt: "A new name, a new identity, and a clearer statement of what you actually race."
---

The championship has been renamed the **MX-5 Mk4 Trophy**, replacing the
MX-5 Cup Great Britain name used previously.

The reason is simple. Every car in the Trophy is a Mk4 Mazda MX-5, built
to one sealed specification, and the name should say so. "Cup Great
Britain" told you where we race. "Mk4 Trophy" tells you what you race,
which matters more when you are sharing a circuit with Mk3 machinery.

The new identity carries a yellow and red treatment across the badge and
wordmark, and it will appear on timing screens, grid boards and car
numbers from the first round.

Nothing else about the plan changes. The Trophy runs under the BRSCC, to
the same technical specification already announced, starting on the MX-5
SuperCup grid in 2027 and taking its own slot on the roster from 2028.
