---
title: "Goodyear named championship tyre partner"
date: 2026-10-19
category: Partners
excerpt: "One control tyre, one supplier, across every round of the season."
---

Goodyear joins the MX-5 Mk4 Trophy as championship tyre partner, supplying
the control tyre for every car on the grid.

A one-make championship only works if the tyre is the same for everyone,
and a single supplier across the season removes the one variable that
quietly decides results in series where teams can shop around. Every
driver will race the same compound, available at every round.

Full technical detail, including compound and the number of sets
permitted per weekend, is published in the technical regulations.
